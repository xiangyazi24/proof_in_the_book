## Ch13 CORRECTION (2026-06-14, ChatGPT adversarial audit of the certificate)

**The `chapter13 : CauchyRigidityCertificate → False` certificate is NOT a faithful encoding of general
Cauchy rigidity** — it is a valid POST-REDUCTION counting lemma for a *connected triangulated strict-sign
sphere*, but it silently combines two incompatible graphs (§3.3 trap):
- vertices want the ACTIVE nonzero-edge graph (only active vertices get Lemma II's ≥4);
- faces want a TRIANGULATION (only triangles give ≤2 per face and `3F=2E`).
These are not the same graph: deleting zero edges from a triangulated sphere merges triangles into
polygons, so `3F=2E` is FALSE on the active graph; `V−E+F=2` hides a connectivity assumption (disconnected
active graph gives `1+c`); and `StrictTriangleSigns` (only ±, no 0) can represent neither zero-edge
triangulated faces nor non-triangular active faces.

So `chapter13` should be read as `chapter13_from_augmented_strict_signed_triangulation`, a special-case
abstraction — NOT the Book theorem. My planned Step 5 (positing Euler/3F=2E/double-count as
`CauchyPolytopePair` fields) would have REINFORCED this trap — abandoned.

**What IS solid and faithful (keep):** all proven geometry — arm lemma, bridges, Lemma II, subArc, and
especially `cauchyArmVertex_of_links` (= ChatGPT's `vertex_link_lower`: an active vertex's two real links
⟹ ≥4 sign changes around the link). That is genuinely the per-vertex lower bound.

**The CORRECT residual** (replaces "construct the certificate"): the *marked-edge combinatorial reduction*
— from a nonzero ±/0 signing of the triangulated polytope surface, prove the discrete Cauchy lemma (some
ACTIVE vertex has 0 or 2 sign changes) / construct the connected auxiliary strict signed triangulation with
the monotone double-count. This is Cauchy's genuine combinatorial content beyond the arm lemma (same family
as Ch35's discrete-Jordan). The certificate must be RESTATED around this (ChatGPT's
`CauchyMarkedTriangulatedSphere` + `cauchy_combinatorial_lemma`). Specific Lean-targetable spec being
nailed down with ChatGPT (the counting-mismatch crux + the zero-diagonal-invariance simplification).
