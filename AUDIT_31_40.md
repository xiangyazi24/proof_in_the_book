# Semantic Audit: Chapters 31-40

Read-only audit against the 17-point standard, especially C-group semantic checks:
statement fidelity, no trivial-true impostors, no fragment impostors, and no escape
through assuming the conclusion or hard half.

| chapter | book intended theorem (1 line) | Lean `chapterNN` statement | verdict | note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 31 | Cayley's formula: the number of labeled trees on `n` vertices is `n^(n-2)`. | `theorem chapter31 (n : Nat) (hn : 2 <= n) : Fintype.card (LabeledTree n) = n ^ (n - 2)` | FAITHFUL | Matches the intended counting theorem for the standard `2 <= n` range. Public theorem supplies the Prufer correspondence internally and does not expose a hard hypothesis. |
| 32 | The chapter compares bijective/algebraic proofs of combinatorial identities: Vandermonde, hockey-stick, and binomial identities. | `theorem chapter32 (m n k : Nat) : (m + n).choose k = sum over antidiagonal k of m.choose i * n.choose j` | FRAGMENT | Faithful to Vandermonde only. The module proves other advertised identities as named lemmas, but the canonical `chapter32` endpoint exposes only one example from the chapter scope. |
| 33 | Latin square completion: any partial Latin square of order `n` with at most `n-1` entries can be completed to a full Latin square. | Given `P`, `usedInCol`, witness/count hypotheses, proves `exists row : Fin n -> Fin n, Function.Injective row and forall j, row j notin usedInCol j`. | FRAGMENT | This is a genuine Hall row-completion step with Hall proved internally, not the full iterative completion theorem for a partial Latin square. No self-conclusion escape, but it is explicitly short of the book theorem. |
| 34 | Galvin/Dinitz theorem: every `n x n` array with color lists of size at least `n` has a proper list Latin coloring. | `theorem chapter34 (lists : Cell n -> Finset alpha) (hlists : forall cell, n <= (lists cell).card) : exists color, DinitzSolution lists color` | FAITHFUL | `DinitzSolution` unfolds to list-respecting plus row/column conflict properness. | 35 | Five color theorem: every planar graph is 5-colorable. | `theorem chapter35 (vertices : Finset alpha) (degree : alpha -> Nat) (hsum : sum degrees < 6 * vertices.card) : exists v in vertices, degree v <= 5` | FRAGMENT | Confirmed known issue. | 36 | Art gallery theorem: every simple polygon with `n` vertices is guardable by `floor(n/3)` vertex guards. | For an abstract `TriangulatedPolygon n S`, proves `exists guards : Finset (Fin n), guards.card <= n / 3` hitting every abstract triangle in `S`. | FRAGMENT | Honest combinatorial core, not the full geometric theorem.
